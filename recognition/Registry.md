# The Saga — Official Recognition Registry

This is the single, authoritative record of every recognition badge granted under
The Saga. A recognition is official if and only if it appears here. See
[Recognition Policy.md](./Recognition%20Policy.md) for what each badge means and how
it is earned.

## ID Format

Each entry receives a unique ID in the form `SAGA-<BADGE>-<NUMBER>`:

| Badge | Prefix |
|---|---|
| Founder & Lead Maintainer | `FND` |
| Saga Fellow | `FEL` |
| Founding Contributor | `FC` |
| Saga Maintainer | `MNT` |
| Saga Contributor | `CON` |

`Scope` records what the recognition applies to — `The Saga` for the whole
initiative, a specific tier (`Tier I`, `Tier II`, `Tier III`, `Tier I–III`), or a named
component (e.g. `Sorophy™`). `Status` is `Active`, `Emeritus`, or `Revoked`.

## Exclusivity and Self-Grants

**Founder & Lead Maintainer is exclusive** — its holder does not also hold any of the
other four badges. See [Recognition Policy.md](./Recognition%20Policy.md#self-grants)
for the full rule.

Any entry granted by an individual to themselves is marked `Self-granted: Yes` below.
This is not a lesser recognition — it simply keeps the record honest about who
approved it.

## Amendments

- Entries are only added by The Saga's Founder & Lead Maintainer, per the criteria in
  the Recognition Policy.
- A `Status` change (e.g. to `Revoked`) is recorded here directly rather than by
  deleting the row — the Registry preserves history rather than erasing it.
- Once more than one Fellow or Maintainer exists, a self-granted Fellow or Founding
  Contributor entry additionally records the countersigning Fellow or Maintainer, per
  [Recognition Policy.md](./Recognition%20Policy.md#self-grants).
