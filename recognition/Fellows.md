# Saga Fellows

Individuals recognized as **Saga Fellow** — see
[Recognition Policy](./Recognition%20Policy.md#2-saga-fellow) for full criteria.

Saga Fellow is the highest recognition The Saga grants, reserved for sustained,
exceptional contribution across all three tiers and material influence on the
project's direction and long-term growth. It is appointment-only and intentionally
rare — it is not awarded for commit count, popularity, financial contribution, or a
single major contribution.
