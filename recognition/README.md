# The Saga — Recognition

This directory documents The Saga's official recognition badges: what they mean, who
qualifies for them, and who currently holds them.

Recognition badges acknowledge meaningful contribution, entrusted responsibility, and
exceptional service to The Saga. They are **recognition only** — not licenses,
permissions, ownership stakes, or employment titles. Holding a badge changes nothing
about a contributor's own copyright, license, or business, and it does not grant any
authority over The Saga beyond what has been explicitly assigned.

## The Badges, at a Glance

| Badge | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Founder & Lead Maintainer | Unique |
| Saga Fellow | Extremely rare |
| Founding Contributor | Very rare |
| Saga Maintainer | Rare |
| Saga Contributor | Uncommon |

These do not form a simple ladder — see [`Recognition Policy.md`](./Recognition%20Policy.md)
for how they relate to one another.

## Authority

Recognition is granted by The Saga — in practice, by Subhradeep Sarkar as Founder &
Lead Maintainer — according to the criteria in the Recognition Policy, and every grant
is recorded in [`Registry.md`](./Registry.md). A badge that does not appear in the
Registry is not officially recognized, regardless of any other claim made about it.
