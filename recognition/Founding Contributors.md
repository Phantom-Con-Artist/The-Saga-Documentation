# Founding Contributors

Individuals recognized as **Founding Contributor** — see
[Recognition Policy](./Recognition%20Policy.md#3-founding-contributor) for full
criteria.

A Founding Contributor made a meaningful contribution during The Saga's foundational
development and helped establish its architecture, implementation, ecosystem, or
direction. Contribution to all three tiers is not required — recognition is based on
historical importance, not breadth.

This category is intended to close once The Saga's founding period has ended.
