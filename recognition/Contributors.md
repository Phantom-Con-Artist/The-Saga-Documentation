# Saga Contributors

Individuals recognized as **Saga Contributor** — see
[Recognition Policy](./Recognition%20Policy.md#5-saga-contributor) for full criteria.

A Saga Contributor has made meaningful contributions across all three tiers of The
Saga — Tier I (The Saga Architecture), Tier II (Sorophy™), and Tier III (Myriad
Ecosystem). Contribution to only one or two tiers does not qualify for this
recognition.
