# Saga Maintainers

Individuals recognized as **Saga Maintainer** — see
[Recognition Policy](./Recognition%20Policy.md#4-saga-maintainer) for full criteria.

A Saga Maintainer is entrusted with ongoing responsibility for maintaining an official
or recognized part of The Saga — a specific scope within Architecture, Sorophy™, or
the Myriad Ecosystem. Maintainer status does not require contribution across all
three tiers, and is a role of responsibility rather than a higher contribution rank.
